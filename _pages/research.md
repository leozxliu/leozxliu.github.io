**We work at the intersection of engineering and medicine.**

Our research is driven by fundamental fluid mechanics and the pathology of life-threatening diseases such as heart attacks and strokes. We share new discoveries through journals such as the [*Journal of Fluid Mechanics*](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-fluid-mechanics) and [*Blood*](https://ashpublications.org/blood). Our current research focuses on four topics:

1. **Scientific Computing and AI**
2. **Physics of Complex Fluids**
3. **Blood Mechanobiology**
4. **Medical Devices**
