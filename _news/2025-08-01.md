**[Grant]** Liu Lab received a research grant from the [Consortium for Medical Marijuana Clinical Outcomes Research](https://mmjoutcomes.org/2025-grants-program/) to develop a miniaturized flow-based point-of-care device for assessing and mitigating the risks of cannabinoid-associated coagulopathy.
