**[Award]** Our work on how blood suspension rheology regulates cellular and molecular mechanobiology (led by Rukiye) received the inaugural Shmuel Einav Award for Mechanobiology at the 10th International Biofluid Mechanics and Mechanobiology Symposium ([IBMS10](https://ibms10.eng.uci.edu/)). IBMS, founded by Shmuel Einav and Mory Gharib at Caltech in 2003, is the most prestigious conference in the field of biofluid mechanics and mechanobiology.
